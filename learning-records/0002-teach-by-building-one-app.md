# Mission shape: one app plus deliberate topic practice

Jay chose to learn by shipping a single Team Task Tracker (Owner/Admin/Member/Viewer) while also practising topics on the side — not pure project work and not pure theory. He chose to hand-write auth rather than use Auth0/Clerk, specifically to learn it, and wants tests only where they matter most: auth and permission rules.

So: every lesson should produce a piece of that one app, and each lesson's practice task should be runnable against it. Do not propose managed-auth shortcuts for weeks 3-6 — the hand-rolling is the goal. Do propose them later as a contrast lesson.

Evidence: choices made during the grilling session on 2026-09-01, captured in [[MISSION.md]] and PLAN.md.
